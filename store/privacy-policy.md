# Stack Puzzles Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Stack Puzzles does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- No accounts. You never sign in or give us a name, email address, or other identifier.
- No tracking or analytics. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs and makes no network requests.
- No permissions. The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos.
- Data on your device. Your level progress, stars, daily puzzle results, and sound, haptics and color-blind settings are saved on your device. If you turn on iCloud Backup or Android backup, your device includes them in its own backup to your iCloud or Google account, the same as other app data. Stack Puzzles never receives or reads those backups. Deleting the app deletes the copy on your device.
- Sharing. If you tap Share after a daily puzzle, your result text goes to the app you pick in your device's share sheet. Stack Puzzles sends nothing itself.

Because Stack Puzzles never sends your data to us or anyone else, there is nothing for us to sell, share, or delete on request.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
