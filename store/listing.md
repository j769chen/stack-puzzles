## Description [4000]

```
Two classic stacking puzzles in one calm, ad-free app, rendered in smooth 3D.

TOWER STACK
The Tower of Hanoi. Move the whole tower to another rod, one disk at a time, never placing a larger disk on a smaller one. Eight levels take you from 3 disks to 10. A move counter shows how close you are to the optimal solution.

RING SORT
Sort the colored rings so every rod holds a single color. Move one ring at a time onto an empty rod or onto a ring of the same color. Levels never run out: colors ramp up from 3 to 10, then the rods grow taller and the challenge starts again. Every level is checked to be solvable before you see it, and the same level number always deals the same rings.

FEATURES
- Undo and restart at any time
- See the fewest moves needed for every Ring Sort level
- Tells you when no moves are left
- Sound effects and haptics, each with its own switch
- Full screen reader support
- Works offline, with no account, ads, or tracking
```
