## Description [4000]

```
Stacking and sorting puzzles in one calm, ad-free app, rendered in smooth 3D.

DAILY PUZZLE
A new Ring Sort puzzle every day, the same for everyone. It starts gentle on Monday and builds to a tough Sunday. Match the fewest possible moves for three stars, keep your streak going, and share your result.

TOWER STACK
The Tower of Hanoi. Move the whole tower to another rod, one disk at a time, never placing a larger disk on a smaller one. Eight levels take you from 3 disks to 10. A move counter shows how close you are to the optimal solution, and the stars you're on track for.

RING SORT
Sort the colored rings so every rod holds a single color. Move one ring at a time onto an empty rod or onto a ring of the same color. Levels never run out: colors ramp up from 3 to 10, then the rods grow taller and the challenge starts again. Every level is checked to be solvable before you see it, and the same level number always deals the same rings. No scores and no timers: just sorting.

TOWER SORT
Ring Sort meets the Tower of Hanoi. Rings come in sizes as well as colors, and a ring only fits on a bigger one. Sort every color onto its own rod, largest at the bottom. Levels grow from 3 colors of 3 sizes to 5 colors of 4, and every one is checked to be solvable.

FEATURES
- Undo and restart at any time
- Stars for Tower Stack and the daily puzzle, with a daily streak
- Tells you when no moves are left
- Sound effects and haptics, each with its own switch
- Full screen reader support
- Color-blind patterns that mark every ring color
- Works offline, with no account, ads, or tracking
```
